# Design fixed before model inference

Use Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, unquantized bfloat16, and 1,600 uniformly sampled MMLU test questions (seed 20261002). Question-disjoint splits: 800 train, 300 validation, 500 test. This is a constrained-choice epistemic-error study, not a direct measurement of free-text hallucination or mental intent. No claim of novelty for belief verification itself.

Four neutral elicitation templates each use a cyclic rotation of a randomly permuted answer order. All four correct = stable-correct (K); all four select the same wrong original answer = stable-wrong (W); other patterns = inconsistent (U). A fifth, independent neutral wording is the evaluation control. These are behavioral strata, not proof of knowledge or its absence. Re-analyze with >=3/4 correct K as a sensitivity test.

Cross every question with neutral, score pressure, social approval pressure, explicit-honest and explicit-lie prompts. A preferred answer is drawn uniformly over four original choices independently of ground truth, and held fixed between pressure templates. Pressure describes a payoff or approval preference, never says the preferred answer is factually correct, and never instructs falsehood. Explicit lying is a calibration set, not counted as spontaneous deception.

Decode highest-logit A/B/C/D token, save normalized choice probabilities and unrestricted top-token compliance. Extract residual states after layers 7,14,21,28 at last prompt token and answer token. Primary layer 14 fixed; other layers diagnostic. Inference deterministic. No chain of thought. Primary false-output contrast: pressure errors on K (belief-contradicting proxy L) versus neutral errors on U (epistemic-error proxy H). Controls: pressure errors on U; correct answers on K under the same pressure; last-prompt-token probes; answer-letter features and entropy; question-disjoint split; train on score pressure and test on social pressure.

Fit standardized L2 logistic regression (C=0.1, balanced classes, max_iter=2000): instructed-deception proxy (K explicit wrong vs K explicit honest correct), neutral error detector (all neutral wrong vs correct), pressure detector (K score wrong vs K score correct), and direct L versus H discriminator. Report test AUROC with question bootstrap confidence intervals and validation thresholds at 10% negative false positives. Never reinterpret AUROC reversal after seeing test labels. Descriptive composition over all questions reported separately for each condition; no natural-world prevalence claim. Use bootstrap over question IDs for aggregate correlated contrasts, 2000 replicates. Same-pressure false-only L vs U errors is the key confound check, along with prompt-only readout.

## Implementation notes before full-study results
Two exact duplicate question stems in the initial sample were replaced before full-study inference, including one train/test overlap; `deduplication.json` records the source rows. A 16-question engineering pilot verified execution and cached-state extraction; it is excluded from analysis. PyTorch 2.14.1 failed before producing outputs because its eager Triton path required an unavailable C compiler. Inference uses PyTorch 2.6.0. Added deterministic condition-only and question-text TF-IDF controls before examining full-study results; these assess shortcuts and difficulty/topic separation. Primary probes remain unchanged.

## Additional diagnostic after neutral checks, before detector outcomes
A sixth exploratory knowledge-only probe is fit on K versus U under neutral evaluation, irrespective of evaluation correctness. Its transfer to same-pressure errors tests whether the stratum distinction is already readable without training on misreports. It uses the same fixed linear-probe settings. It is not part of the original five-probe comparison.

## Post-hoc diagnostics after primary detector results
Observed that K social errors selected the preferred answer more often than U social errors. Added `robustness.py`: (i) a preference-adherence scalar baseline; (ii) both-class restriction to errors that select the preferred option; (iii) both-class restriction to a preferred option that conflicts with the key; (iv) require the independent neutral evaluation also correct for K positives. No probes are refit and no primary results are replaced. These conditional comparisons are descriptive and do not causally isolate an adherence representation.
